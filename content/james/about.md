James Manon-og is a full-stack developer based in Davao City, Philippines. He works
remotely, in the UTC+8 time zone.

He works mainly in TypeScript, Python and Java. In his own summary, most of his work has
been API and integration heavy: payment and email provider integrations, webhook
handling, multi-step workflows, and moving manual operational steps into code.

In his own words, he can work across full-stack, mobile, DevOps, UI/UX and machine
learning, and his goal now is to grow to a senior engineering level.

He studied for a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science at Ateneo de Davao University
from August 2022 to May 2026. During that time he was a Full Stack Developer at SAMAHAN
Systems Development (SYSDEV) and served as a
Class President on the AdDU Council of Class Presidents in 2024 to 2025.

His professional experience includes internships at Symph, where he worked on the Lesson
Planner platform, and at Orange & Bronze Software Labs, where he did backend work on
fintech software.
